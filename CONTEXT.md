# Portfolio

Rafal Chorazewicz's personal website. It presents his work to people who want to see what he has built.

## Language

**Rafal Chorazewicz**:
The person this site is about. The only public identity.
_Avoid_: Lime (as a brand), Forsberg, Liam, studio name, rafaldev (as the site's public name)

**Mark**:
A geometric split sign, two mirrored arcs with padding so nothing clips. It sits in the loader, opens on load, and eases shut on scroll. Original drawing, not letters, not leftover Lime geometry.
_Avoid_: the salmon block logo, dropping the split, bisecting an RC monogram, cloning the Obys or Yulia marks

**Portfolio**:
This website. A personal site whose job is to show Rafal's work.
_Avoid_: agency site, friend's site, Lime portfolio (as a public name)

**Project**:
A piece of Rafal's work on the Work strip. It has a public artifact: a live site or a repository. Clicking the image opens a case view on the Portfolio. The right side is a scroll stack of stills. The artifact URL is a link inside that view.
_Avoid_: job (for strip items), opening the live site on first click, embedded live preview, fade slideshows

**Job**:
Paid employment. It belongs on About as history, not on the Work strip, unless that Job produced a public artifact Rafal may show.
_Avoid_: treating Job and Project as the same thing

**Work**:
The homepage strip of Projects, in this order: Sagobo, Job Hunter, Bury Meble, Frisör. Frames are portrait. The image is a portrait-framed capture of the live site's hero, shot at a 3:4 viewport so nothing is cut off (see `scripts/capture.mjs`). The Mark opens around that frame. Job Hunter uses a designed poster.
_Avoid_: a list of employers, a CV, fake product UI, landscape hero screenshots in the strip, cropping a landscape screenshot into a portrait frame

**About**:
The page for Rafal's story: Introduction, Work, Jobs, Tekniklärare, and BTH. No numbered section labels. No skill grid. No off-duty hobbies. No freelance ask.
_Avoid_: six years of experience, HAVE A PROJECT, Roleplaying & Indie Tunes, 01 / Work style labels

**Contact**:
The only public contact is rafal.chorazewicz@icloud.com. No phone. No portrait.
_Avoid_: Gmail, rafaldev.se as a mailbox, phone number, photo from the private profile

**Sagobo**:
Rafal's live product: personalized children's books in Swedish, with the child as the hero. A Project. Canonical URL: https://sagobo.se (sagobo.se serves the same app; it is not a second product).
_Avoid_: Saogobo, Segobo, treating sagobo.se as a different Project

**Job Hunter**:
Rafal's open-source tool at https://github.com/Rafalskky/job-hunter. It reads Arbetsförmedlingen's public JobSearch API, scores ads, and writes tailored CV and letter PDFs. A Project. Its artifact is the repository. On Work it appears as a designed poster.
_Avoid_: JobHunter as a live website, fake dashboard, poll

**Bury Meble**:
Rafal's live site at https://burymeble.com: custom furniture (kitchens, wardrobes, bathrooms). A Project. Its artifact is the live site.
_Avoid_: Bury Möbler, Bury Meble as a Job

**Frisör**:
Rafal's live site at https://frisor-five.vercel.app: a hair-salon site ("Vi skapar stilar som håller"). A Project. Its artifact is the live site.
_Avoid_: Barbershop, inventing a client name

**Frilans Finans**:
Current Job (2024 – present). Frontend for financial services. About only.
_Avoid_: putting Frilans Finans on Work

**Marknadsinformation**:
Job (2023 – 2024). Frontend, including a flagship-site redesign. About only.
_Avoid_: Marknads information, putting it on Work

**Brixo**:
Job (2020 – 2021). Frontend; Angular to React migration. About only.
_Avoid_: Brixo, putting it on Work

**Tekniklärare**:
Job (Dec 2021 – Sep 2023). One line on About. Not a Project.
_Avoid_: putting teaching on Work, omitting the 2021–2023 gap

**BTH**:
Blekinge Tekniska Högskola, Game Programming, 2018 – 2021. One line on About. The source of the motion and interaction craft, not a Job.
_Avoid_: treating BTH as employment
